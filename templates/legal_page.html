This policy describes what information is handled when you use ReelSaver and how it is used.

When you request video information or a download, the video URL and your selected quality are sent to our server to fulfill that request. The server contacts the relevant platform to retrieve available information or video data. The app does not require an account and does not save submitted URLs in an application database.

Temporary processing

A requested download may be processed in a temporary server directory so it can be sent to you. Temporary files are removed after a completed or failed download, subject to unexpected server interruptions or technical limitations.
